个人简介

About

药学、化学与
医学转化

Pharmacy, chemistry, and clinical translation

本科就读药学专业；硕士阶段从事环肽全合成、结构设计与抗炎活性评价， 系统掌握了从分子设计、化学合成到生物学评价的完整研究链条。

2021年进入南京大学化学化工学院攻读有机化学博士学位， 研究蛋白-蛋白相互作用中的界面识别与功能调控，并探索其工程化应用。

2025年7月加入宁波大学附属第一医院中心实验室，任特聘副研究员、课题组长。 课题组以金属相关机制与工程平台为主线，聚焦跟腱损伤、口腔疾病、肿瘤靶向与PET/CT分子影像等临床问题。

I trained in pharmacy as an undergraduate. My master's research covered the total synthesis, structural design, and anti-inflammatory evaluation of cyclic peptides, which gave me end-to-end training from molecular design and synthesis to biological assessment.

In 2021 I began a Ph.D. in organic chemistry at Nanjing University, studying interface recognition and functional regulation in protein-protein interactions and exploring how they can be engineered.

Since July 2025 I have been a Distinguished Associate Research Fellow and independent PI at the Central Laboratory of The First Affiliated Hospital of Ningbo University. Built around metal-centered mechanisms and engineering platforms, the group works on tendon injury, oral disease, cancer targeting, and PET/CT molecular imaging.

研究方向

Research

金属机制、工程平台与分子诊疗

Metal mechanisms, engineering, and theranostics

我们从临床问题出发，研究金属离子、配位结构、金属界面与放射金属， 并把机制认识转化为局部递送、组织修复和分子诊疗工具。
主要合作科室为骨科中心、口腔科和核医学科。

Starting from clinical questions, we study metal ions, coordination structures, metal interfaces, and radiometals, and turn what we learn into tools for local delivery, tissue repair, and molecular theranostics.
Our main clinical partners are orthopedics, stomatology, and nuclear medicine.

McMetal · Mechanism

金属作为机制变量

Metals as mechanistic variables

探究病理性结晶、金属离子稳态与组织微环境如何影响炎症、矿化和组织修复。

How do pathological crystals, metal-ion homeostasis, and the tissue microenvironment shape inflammation, mineralization, and repair?

  • MSU晶体、牙结石、动脉粥样硬化等病理性矿化过程
  • 金属组学与金属离子细胞生物学
  • 合作科室：骨科中心
  • Pathological mineralization, including MSU crystals, dental calculus, and atherosclerosis
  • Metallomics and metal-ion cell biology
  • Clinical partner: Orthopedics Center
The mouse metallomic landscape of aging and metabolism
EnMetal · Engineering

金属作为工程节点

Metals as engineering nodes

面向跟腱损伤与组织修复，开发基于金属配位、金属多酚网络和MOF/纳米酶的局部递送平台。

Injectable and local delivery platforms built on metal coordination, metal-phenolic networks, MOFs, and nanozymes, aimed at tendon injury and tissue repair.

  • 水凝胶、3D打印支架、静电纺丝体系
  • 微针、微球等递送缓释系统
  • 合作科室：骨科中心、口腔科
  • Hydrogels, 3D-printed scaffolds, and electrospun fibers
  • Microneedle- and microsphere-based sustained delivery systems
  • Clinical partners: Orthopedics Center, Stomatology
Nanozymes: Rugged catalysis for diagnostics and enzyme-replacement therapy
TxMetal · Theranostics

金属作为诊疗工具

Metals as theranostic tools

面向肿瘤靶向与PET/CT分子影像，开发放射金属配位、多肽及binder设计工具。

Radiometal chemistry, metal coordination, and binder design, developed into tools for cancer targeting and PET/CT molecular imaging.

  • PPI界面肽段、从头设计binder与酵母展示
  • 基于MOF的PET/CT成像示踪剂
  • 合作科室：核医学科、口腔科
  • PPI-interface peptides, de novo binder design, and yeast display
  • MOF-based PET/CT imaging probes
  • Clinical partners: Nuclear Medicine, Stomatology
The past, present and future of de novo protein design ProteinBase
